About MachiVerse ABOUT

Not a finished world,
but the process by which a world becomes itself.

A city may be here not because someone placed it there from the start. Water made the land livable. People gathered. Work emerged. Roads followed. The city expanded, and what once was change became a familiar landscape to the next generation.

MachiVerse aims to treat cities, residents, and environments not as static finished objects, but as states shaped by past conditions and choices, with present changes continuing into the future.

Not merely “building a world,”
but simulating why the world became what it is.

SYSTEM

The world we aim for VISION

A world changes as a result of
many things being connected.

01

Nature changes how people live

Terrain, water, and vegetation are intended to be world states that influence settlement, production, industry, and transportation rather than mere scenery.

02

Human activity creates society

Individual lives and choices can connect into families, work, organizations, education, and other relationships that alter the social state.

03

Economy changes the shape of cities

Resources, production, logistics, markets, and movement are intended to connect so that their accumulated effects appear in towns and cities.

04

Change remains as history

Events are not meant to vanish after they happen. Past states should influence later states, leaving reasons that can be traced in the present.

From the developer DEVELOPER'S NOTE

Mio NagumoSystem Developer

I want the reasons behind
the present to remain traceable.

If a world retains not only the result of change but also the conditions and events that led there, it becomes possible to trace why the present looks the way it does. That kind of continuity through time is what I want MachiVerse to preserve.

DIVER

Diver PARTICIPATION

Not above the world,
but inside it as one resident.

MachiVerse calls its participant role the “Diver.” The long-term goal is not an omnipotent outside operator, but a resident who participates within the world's rules, time, social relationships, and reactions from others.

A Diver's actions can become events in the world, propagate through their surroundings, and eventually become part of the world's history.
