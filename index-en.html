GUIDE

First, the idea INTRODUCTION

What should a “world simulation”
actually simulate?

I don’t want MachiVerse to begin with finished cities placed into a map. Water makes land livable. People gather. Work appears. Roads follow. A city emerges. I want those accumulated reasons to be part of the world itself.

WORLD

How the world works CONNECTED WORLD

I don’t want the environment
and the people to be separate systems.

Mountains and rivers are not just scenery. They affect settlement, movement, and production. Human choices change society, and social or economic change comes back into the shape of cities. MachiVerse aims to keep those relationships connected.

01
Nature → Life

Terrain, water, and vegetation influence settlement, industry, and transportation.

02
People → Society

Lives and choices connect into family, work, organizations, and other relationships.

03
Economy → City

Resources, production, logistics, markets, and movement appear in urban form.

04
Change → History

Past states remain relevant instead of being discarded after each event.

TIME

Time and history TIME & HISTORY

I don’t want to save
only the world’s present state.

Every city in the present has earlier states behind it. Events happen, choices are made, and the next state follows. I want that process to remain part of the world. That is why causality and time matter as much as state in MachiVerse.

Environment→Residents→Society→Economy→City→History
DIVER

Diver PARTICIPATION

I build the system from outside.
A Diver goes inside it.

This distinction matters. I’m a developer on the real-world side of MachiVerse. A Diver is different: not an omnipotent operator placing anything from above, but a participant living inside the world’s rules, time, and social relationships.

Mio Nagumo ≠ Diver Mio is a system developer on the real-world side. A Diver is the participant role that enters the simulated world.

Alpha 1.0 already includes a minimal Diver operation flowing from General View through Gateway to Simulation Core, with confirmed WorldState returning to the View.

STATUS

Current development status ALPHA 1.0

It isn’t finished yet.
But it is no longer only a concept.

MachiVerse is currently at Alpha 1.0. The full large-scale vision is not implemented yet. But the first end-to-end loop — view the world, issue an operation, change authoritative state, and receive the result — is already connected across real components.

Alpha 1.0

Reproducible vertical slice

Simulation Core, Gateway, General View, and Administration View are connected, including a minimal participation operation, FULL/DELTA state synchronization, persistence, and recovery paths.
